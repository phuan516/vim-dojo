  ciw / diw / yiw     change / delete / yank the word under the cursor
  ci" ci' ci`         change what is inside the quotes
  ci( ci[ ci{         change what is inside the brackets
  ca( ci( difference  a = around, includes the brackets themselves
  cit                 change inside an HTML/JSX tag
  .                   repeat the last change
  *                   search for the word under the cursor
  n  N                next / previous match
  cgn                 change the next match of the last search, and
                      then  .  repeats "find next and change it" -
                      this is the real multi-cursor replacement
