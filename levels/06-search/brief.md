  Ctrl+F in VS Code opens a little box. In vim, search IS a motion:
  /thing jumps there, and because it is a motion you can also say
  d/thing to delete everything up to it.

  The one you will use most is  *  : put the cursor on any word and
  press *, and vim searches for that exact word. No typing, no box.
