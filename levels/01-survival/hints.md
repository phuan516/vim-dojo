The typo is a swap: "dya" should be "day". Put the cursor on the
wrong character and press x to delete it, then a to append the
letter back in the right place. Esc when done.
---
To kill a line, put the cursor anywhere on it and press dd. The
whole line disappears, no selecting required.
---
Press o on the line above where you want the new text. It opens a
blank line below and drops you into INSERT mode in one keystroke.
Then type the line, press Esc, then :wq and Enter.
