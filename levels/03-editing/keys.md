  dd      delete (cut) a line        3dd  three lines
  yy      yank (copy) a line         3yy  three lines
  p  P    put below / put above the cursor
  o  O    open a line below / above and start typing
  x       delete one character       X  delete backwards
  cw      change to end of word (deletes it, drops you in INSERT)
  C       change from cursor to end of line
  D       delete from cursor to end of line
  r       replace exactly one character, stay in NORMAL
  u       undo
