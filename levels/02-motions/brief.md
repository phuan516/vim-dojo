  Arrow keys work in vim. They are also a trap: they keep your hand
  off home row and they only ever move one character. Vim's real
  motions move by word, line, paragraph, screen and file.

  Two files are open. Read order.provider.ts, answer the five
  questions in answers.txt, save both.

  Rule of this level: no arrow keys, no mouse. The checker can tell.

  Switch between the two open files with  :bn  (buffer next) - you
  will learn that properly at level 10.
