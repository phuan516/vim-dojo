  Every jump you make - a search, a G, a % - is recorded. Ctrl-O walks
  back through that history and Ctrl-I walks forward again. It is
  Alt+Left / Alt+Right, and it works across files.

  This is the fix for "I jumped to look at something and now I have
  lost my place".

  In order.provider.ts, answer the three bracket questions.

  Then, for practice that nobody is grading: put the cursor somewhere
  in the middle, press ma to drop a mark called a, press G to go to
  the bottom, then 'a to come back. Do it a few times. Marks and
  Ctrl-O are what let you roam a file fearlessly.
