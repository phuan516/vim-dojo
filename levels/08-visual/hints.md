Put the cursor on the i of id (line 2, column 3). Press Ctrl-V, then
jjj to stretch the block down four lines. It looks like a thin
column - that is correct.
---
With the block showing, press I (capital i), type  readonly  with a
trailing space, then Esc. The text appears on all four lines the
moment you press Esc, not before. Wait for it.
---
For the semicolons: cursor on line 2, Ctrl-V, jjj, then $ to run the
block to the end of every line however long it is, then A ; Esc .
---
For the strings: cursor on the p of pending, then  gUiw  - "make
upper case, inner word". Then j and . on the next two.
